# Task Requirements

## Task 1. Expression Evaluation

Calculate the value of $X$ using the formula:

$$X = \frac{2.3 + A \cdot B^2}{0.4 + C \cdot e^D}$$

* **Input Data:** values of variables $A$, $B$, $C$, and $D$ (entered via keyboard).
* **Result:** calculated value of $X$.

## Task 2. Calculating the Area of a Triangle

Calculate the area of a triangle given its three sides $a$, $b$, and $c$, entered from the keyboard.

* **Heron's Formula:**
  $$p = \frac{a + b + c}{2}$$
  $$S = \sqrt{p(p - a)(p - b)(p - c)}$$

* **Input Data:** side lengths of the triangle $a$, $b$, and $c$.
* **Result:** semi-perimeter $p$ and triangle area $S$.
